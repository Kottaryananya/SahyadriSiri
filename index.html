SahyadriSiri

A specialized water reporting and monitoring app for the Sahyadri (Western Ghats) region.

Live Project
  • 📍 Real-time Mapping: View water health across the Sahyadri region.
  • 💧 Community Reporting: Report water clarity, flow, and pollution.

  • 📚 Water Wiki: Learn about local water conservation.
